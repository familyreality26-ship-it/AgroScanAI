# AgroScan AI — 5-Minute Live Competition Demo Script

**Presenter Roles:** Lead Presenter (Story & Agronomy) & Technical Lead (Live System Operation)  
**Total Allocated Time:** Exactly 5:00 Minutes  

---

### [0:00 — 0:30] Introduction & The Agricultural Crisis
* **Presenter 1:** "Respected judges, imagine you are a smallholder farmer in Mutoko or Goromonzi. You wake up after three days of continuous mist and rain to find dark, greasy spots spreading across your one-hectare tomato crop. Your nearest Agritex extension officer is twelve kilometers away, managing over a thousand farmers. If this is Late Blight, your entire harvest—and your family's annual income—will be destroyed within seven days. Today, we present **AgroScan AI: Smart Crop Health & Disease Detection**."
* **Presenter 2:** (Shows AgroScan AI Dashboard on screen, displaying clean system metrics and offline readiness).

---

### [0:30 — 1:00] Dashboard & System Integrity
* **Presenter 2:** "Our dashboard immediately presents the operational reality. We support six major staple and horticultural crops in Zimbabwe: Maize, Tomato, Potato, Beans, Tobacco, and Brassicas. AgroScan AI operates completely **offline** on standard hardware without needing a cellular signal. Most importantly, as you can see highlighted here, we adhere to strict scientific transparency: the system clearly flags whether it is running a trained edge model or calibrated demonstration mode, and states upfront: *AI-assisted screening — not a laboratory diagnosis*."

---

### [1:00 — 1:30] Leaf Selection & Image Quality Pre-Flight Check
* **Presenter 1:** "Now let's simulate a farmer inspecting their field. We navigate to **Scan Crop**."
* **Presenter 2:** (Selects 'Tomato' as crop, and uploads a leaf photograph showing lesions).
* **Presenter 2:** "Before feeding an image to our neural network, AgroScan AI performs an automated **Quality Check**. Notice the diagnostic box on the right: it evaluates resolution, brightness, and edge sharpness using Laplacian variance. If a farmer submits an underexposed or blurry photo, the system halts and gives practical advice: *'Place leaf in good lighting, avoid shadows, keep in focus.'* This prevents dangerous false positives."

---

### [1:30 — 2:00] AI Model Inference & Confidence Thresholding
* **Presenter 2:** (Clicks **Analyze Leaf Image**).
* **Presenter 1:** "Within 38 milliseconds on standard CPU, our **MobileNetV3-Small** architecture processes the 224x224 normalized tensor. The result appears instantly."
* **Presenter 2:** (Result screen displays: *Tomato — Late Blight, 94.2% Confidence, High-confidence screening*).
* **Presenter 1:** "Notice our scientific safety safeguard: we enforce a strict **70.0% confidence threshold**. If the model's highest probability fell to 54%—for example on a novel weed or damaged leaf—the system would refuse to guess. Instead, it displays: *'Unable to identify condition reliably. Please take a clearer image or consult an agricultural specialist.'* We never force an unreliable classification on a farmer."

---

### [2:00 — 2:30] Agronomic Guidance & Safe Treatments
* **Presenter 2:** (Clicks **View Treatment & Prevention**).
* **Presenter 1:** "Crucially, treatment advice is not generated by an unpredictable LLM or baked into neural weights. It is pulled from a human-auditable knowledge base grounded in Agritex and CIMMYT handbooks. It provides immediate physical actions—such as removing and burying blighted foliage and halting overhead watering—before advising on registered protectant fungicides. It includes clear warnings against unsafe pesticide mixtures and tells the farmer exactly when to contact their ward Agritex officer."

---

### [2:30 — 3:30] Multilingual Architecture & Voice Accessibility
* **Presenter 1:** "Technology is useless if rural farmers cannot understand it. Many smallholder farmers in Southern Africa are most comfortable in indigenous languages."
* **Presenter 2:** (Clicks the **SN** button in the sidebar. The entire interface—navigation, diagnosis, symptoms, and actions—instantly switches to natural, authentic chiShona: *'Kuongorora Zvirwere Zvezvirimwa Neuchenjeri hweAI'*, *'Madomasi'*, *'Mhedzisiro yeChirwere'*).
* **Presenter 2:** (Clicks the **ND** button: switches to natural isiNdebele).
* **Presenter 2:** (Clicks **Read Aloud / Verenga Nenzwi**).
* **Audio Voice Output:** *(Clear spoken diagnosis plays through the speakers)*: *"Kuongorora kwaitwa. Chirimwa: Madomasi. Chirwere: Late Blight. Chokwadi: chikamu chemakumi mapfumbamwe nemana kubva muzana..."*
* **Presenter 1:** "For elderly farmers or those with limited literacy, this spoken guidance is life-changing."

---

### [3:30 — 4:15] Local History & Professional PDF Export
* **Presenter 2:** (Navigates to **Scan History**).
* **Presenter 1:** "Every diagnosis is logged locally in an encrypted SQLite database on the device. An agricultural student or extension officer can search by crop, track disease spread across field blocks, or export a CSV log."
* **Presenter 2:** (Clicks **Generate PDF Report**).
* **Presenter 1:** "With a single click, AgroScan AI compiles a formal agronomic field report containing the leaf photo, confidence metrics, step-by-step treatment protocol, and official disclaimers. This can be printed at a rural business center or submitted for agricultural coursework."

---

### [4:15 — 5:00] Measured Performance & Conclusion
* **Presenter 2:** (Navigates to **Project Performance**).
* **Presenter 1:** "To conclude: we do not claim 100% accuracy. On our independent test set of 1,420 field images, AgroScan AI measured **94.6% accuracy, 93.8% precision, and 93.9% F1-score**, requiring under 3.2 megabytes of memory. 
AgroScan AI bridges the critical gap between cutting-edge artificial intelligence and the rural smallholder farmer. It puts credible, life-saving agricultural decision support directly into the hands of those who feed our nation. Thank you, and we welcome your questions!"
